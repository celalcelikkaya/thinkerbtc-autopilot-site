Questions

Before you start

Can ThinkerBTC withdraw my money?

No. Your key only allows reading and futures trading. Withdrawals stay off on Binance, so funds can't leave your account.

Can I stop the bot?

Yes. Pause it from the app and it stops opening trades. Open positions keep their stop loss and take profit, or you can close them yourself.

What happens when my plan ends?

The bot stops opening new trades. Positions that are already open keep their exchange orders until they close.

How do I pay?

In USDC on the Solana network. Scan the QR code with Phantom, Solflare or any Solana Pay wallet. Every QR carries a unique reference for your order, so your payment is recognised and your plan starts automatically, usually within seconds. Network fees on Solana are a fraction of a cent.

Why only Google or Apple sign-in?

So there is no ThinkerBTC password that could leak or be guessed. Your account gets the protection you already have on Google or Apple, including two-step verification. Signing in never gives access to your Binance funds.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

No. ThinkerBTC runs on its own servers and trades through your Binance API key around the clock. Open the dashboard whenever you want to check on it.

Risk Disclosure

ThinkerBTC is software that places trades on your own exchange account using rules you switch on. Trading leveraged crypto futures is one of the riskiest things you can do with money. Read this in full. By ticking the box you confirm you understand it and accept these risks.

1. Not financial advice

ThinkerBTC is not a broker, adviser, fund manager or exchange, and it does not know your personal or financial situation. Decide for yourself whether automated futures trading suits you, and get independent advice if you are unsure.

2. You can lose money, fast

Crypto prices can move sharply within seconds. Futures use leverage: at 10×, a 1% price move against a position changes its result by about 10% of the margin, and a move of roughly 10% can wipe out the margin of that position. Several positions can lose at the same time. You may lose part or all of the money you put into futures trading. Only use money you can afford to lose without changing your life.

3. No promise of profit

ThinkerBTC does not guarantee any profit, win rate or return. Past results, examples, charts, demos and backtests do not predict future results. Markets change, and a strategy that worked before can stop working. Any figure you see on this site that is not from your own account is an illustration.

4. Stop loss, take profit and profit lock have limits

ThinkerBTC places a stop loss and take profit on the exchange for each trade and may move the stop to lock in profit. These orders reduce risk but cannot remove it. In fast or thin markets, during gaps, outages or extreme volatility, an order can fill at a worse price than set (slippage), fill late, be rejected by the exchange, or not fill at all. A position can also be liquidated by the exchange before a stop is reached.

5. Exchange and technical risk

The bot depends on your exchange, its API, internet connections and our servers. Any of these can fail, slow down, change their rules or go offline. Exchanges can restrict accounts, delist pairs, change fees or margin rules, or suffer hacks or insolvency. ThinkerBTC has no control over your exchange and cannot get funds back that an exchange loses or freezes. Software can contain errors despite testing.

6. Your API keys and account security

Create keys that allow reading and futures trading only. Keep withdrawals switched off and restrict the key to our IP address. Never share your exchange password, two-factor codes or withdrawal rights with anyone, including us. You are responsible for your exchange account and its security. You can remove the key on the exchange at any time, which stops the bot from trading.

7. Your settings, your responsibility

You choose the margin per trade and how many trades may be open at once. Larger margin and more open trades mean larger possible losses. Check your settings, keep enough funds in your futures wallet, and watch your account. Pausing the bot stops new trades; open positions keep their exchange orders until they close or you close them yourself.

8. Fees and costs

Your subscription is charged whether trades win or lose. Your exchange separately charges trading fees and funding fees on open futures positions, which reduce results. Prices for plans are shown before you pay.

9. Who can use ThinkerBTC

You must be at least 18 and legally allowed to trade crypto futures where you live. Some countries restrict or ban these products or automated trading. Checking and following your local laws, and reporting and paying any taxes, is your responsibility.

10. Limits of our responsibility
